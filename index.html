Armory Intel

Audit-Ready Firearm & Inventory Tracking for Law Enforcement

Armory Intel gives police departments a secure, audit-ready system to track every firearm, asset, and round of ammunition across their armory. Purpose-built for armorers and quartermasters, it turns inventory chaos into accountability — with the reports, budgets, and qualification records you need at your fingertips.

  • Firearm & Asset Tracking — Track every firearm, serial number, assignment, and round of ammunition with a complete chain-of-custody history
  • Audit-Ready Reports — Generate inspection-ready audit reports on demand to satisfy oversight, accreditation, and compliance requirements
  • Budgeting & Procurement — Plan and track equipment budgets, purchases, and lifecycle costs across your department
  • Qualification Tracking — Monitor officer firearm qualifications, certifications, and expiration dates so no one falls out of compliance
  • Armorer & Quartermaster Dashboard — A single command dashboard giving armorers and quartermasters full visibility into inventory, assignments, and readiness
  • Secure & Encrypted — Military-grade encryption and US-based infrastructure keep your department's data protected and sovereign

Armory AI

Your collection, documented.

Armory AI is an iPhone app for documenting your personal firearms collection. Snap a photo and AI fills in the make, model and caliber — firearms, ammo and gear organized in minutes instead of a weekend.

  • Snap to Identify — Take a photo and AI suggests the manufacturer, model, type and caliber. Review it, fix anything, save.
  • Batch Photo Import — Catalog a whole safe at once from the photos already in your camera roll
  • Insurance-Ready PDF Report — One clean report with photos, serial numbers and values for your insurer
  • Claim Kit — If something is lost or stolen, build a claim packet in a few taps, ready for your insurer or a police report
  • Private by Design — Face ID app lock. Your data is never sold, and your photos are never used to train AI
  • Built in the USA — Designed and built by American Code
